CONSTRUCTION LOW POLY ASSETS

Separate FBX models, English names, metric scale, ground origin.

Excavator.fbx          Tracked excavator with boom, hydraulics and bucket.
Tower_Crane.fbx        Static tower crane with lattice jib and suspended hook.
Concrete_Mixer_Truck.fbx  Six-wheel concrete mixer truck.
Scaffolding.fbx        Two-level scaffold with platforms, braces and ladder.
Brick_Pile.fbx         Pallet of bricks with loose bricks.
Cement_Bags.fbx        Pallet of stacked cement bags.
Timber_Stack.fbx       Strapped timber bundle.
Steel_Beam_Stack.fbx   Stack of I-section steel beams.

All materials use solid colors; no external textures are required.
Meshes are separate children of a named root. No animations, rig or colliders.
FBX excludes preview camera and lights. Import scale: 1.
For URP/HDRP, extract materials and convert them to the project's shader.
Construction_LowPoly.blend contains the complete arranged source scene.
